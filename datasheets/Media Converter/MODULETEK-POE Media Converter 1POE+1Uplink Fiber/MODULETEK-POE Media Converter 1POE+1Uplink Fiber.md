
## POE Media Converter 1 POE+1 Uplink Fiber

The POE switch provides power and data transmission via Cat cables. 1 POE port supply power at the standard of IEEE802.3af/at. Auto-sensing function can supply power to PD device (IEEE802.3af/at standard). If the PoE device disconnected, it would stop supplying power. The POE switch features simple and reliable design, automatic identification of PoE supply voltage and power, speed, full duplex and Auto Uplink cable type.

This cost-effective switch can apply to wireless access point (AP) and IP-based surveillance network solutions.

- ◆ IEEE 802.3af /at power on up to 1 port
