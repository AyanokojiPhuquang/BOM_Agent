## MODULETEK: SFP-FC16G-SW-C10

14.025Gb/s SFP+ Short Wavelength (850nm) Transceiver

## Overview

ModuleTek's SFP-FC16G-SW-C10 SFP+ optical transceiver module is designed for fibre-optic links with data rates up to 14.025Gb/s on MMF. Compliant with FC-PI-5 Rev.6.00 、 SFF-8472 Rev11.00 、 SFF-8402 、 SFF-8432 、 SFF-8431 Rev.4.1. The product implements digital diagnostics via a 2-wire serial bus ,compliant with the SFF-8472 standard.

## Digital Diagnostic Functions

SFP-FC16G-SW-C10  supports  the  2-wire  serial  communication  protocol  as  defined  in  SFF8472.  Digital  diagnostic  information  is  accessible  over  the  2-wire  interface  at  the  address  0xA2. Digital diagnostics for SFP10-SR-C10 are internally calibrated by default. The internal micro control unit accesses the device operating parameters in real time. Such as transceiver temperature, laser bias current, transmitted optical power, received optical power and transceiver supply voltage. The module implements the alarm function of the SFF-8472.

## Functions Description

Sheet

Datasheet MODULETEK's SFP-FC16G-SW-C10 module is manufactured using an advanced COB (Chip On  Board)  process  and  consists  of  a  microcontroller,  a  transmitter-end  optical  engine  and  a receiving-end optical engine.

The microcontroller communicates with the host via a 2-wire serial communication interface, providing control functions, status reporting and monitoring functions (DOM).

Rate selection control: Rx and Tx rates are controlled by pins 7 (RS0) and 9 (RS1) respectively, and product rate selection functions are implemented in the following table ：

| RS0 Input   | RS0 Input      |              |               | RS1 Input   | RS1 Input      |              |               |
|-------------|----------------|--------------|---------------|-------------|----------------|--------------|---------------|
| RS0 Pins    | A2h. 110. bit3 | Rx Rate      | Rx CDR Status | RS1 Pin s   | A2h. 118. bit3 | Tx Rate      | Tx CDR Status |
| 0           | 0              | 4.25/8.5Gb/s | CDR Bypass    | 0           | 0              | 4.25/8.5Gb/s | CDR Bypass    |
| 0           | 1              | 14.025Gb/s   | CDR enable    | 0           | 1              | 14.025Gb/s   | CDR enable    |
| 1           | 0              | 14.025Gb/s   | CDR enable    | 1           | 0              | 14.025Gb/s   | CDR enable    |
| 1           | 1              | 14.025Gb/s   | CDR enable    | 1           | 1              | 14.025Gb/s   | CDR enable    |

The transmitter optical engine consists of a transmitter clock data recovery circuit (CDR) and a laser  driver  circuit  (LD),  a  VCSEL  laser,  and  a  detection  of  photodiodes  (MPD).  The  high-speed differential electrical signal output by the host is reshaped by the recovery of the CDR, fed into the laser driver to amplify, which drives the VCSEL laser to produce a light signal, which is coupled to the fiber by an optical lens. The optical engine integrates a photodiode for the detection of optical power detection, and the laser driver uses an automatic optical power control loop to ensure the stability of the transmitted optical power.

The  receive  optical  engine  consists  of  a  photodiode  (PIN),  a  signal  amplifier  (TIA/LA)  and  a receiving end clock data recovery circuit (CDR), where the optical signal in the optical fiber is coupled by  an  optical  lens  to  the  receiving  photodiode  (PIN)  to  convert  to  a  photoelectric  current,  and  the optical current signal is enhanced by the amplifier. The CDR circuit is fed and clock and data  signal recovery is completed, and finally output to the host in the form of a high-speed differential signal. The microcontroller reads the light intensity received by the photodiode and reports the loss of the received signal if it falls below the set threshold.
