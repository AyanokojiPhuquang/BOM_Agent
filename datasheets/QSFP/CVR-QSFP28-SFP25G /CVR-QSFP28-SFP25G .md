
## DATASHEET CVR-QSFP28-SFP25G 100G QSFP28 to 25G SFP28 Converter

## Features

- ⚫ Trouble-free installation and network bring-up
- ⚫ Compliant to industry standards standards:SFF-8665
- ⚫ IEEE802.3bj/ SFF-8432 IEEE-802.3
- ⚫ Precision process control for minimization of pair-to-pair skew
- ⚫ 1 independent duplex channels operating at 25Gbps,
- ⚫ also support for 5Gbps, 10Gbps data rates
- ⚫ All-metal housing for superior EMI performance
- ⚫ 100 ohm differential impedance system
- ⚫ Operating case temperature: -20 to 85 ℃
- ⚫ Built-in digital diagnostic functions
- ⚫ Low insertion loss
- ⚫ Low crosstalk
- ⚫ Secure latching mechanism
- ⚫ RoHS compliant
