## MODULETEK: QSFP-100G-ER4

100G QSFP28 ER4 Optical Transceiver

## QSFP-100G-ER4 Overview

ModuleTek's QSFP-100G-ER4 QSFP28 ER4 optical transceivers are based on 100G Ethernet IEEE 802.3bm standard. QSFP28 ER4 offers 4 independent transmit and receive channels, each capable of 25G for an aggregate bandwidth of 100G.

## Features

- Supports 100GBASE-ER4;
- Lane bit rate 106.25 Gb/s with PAM4;
- Up to 40km transmission on SMF;
- 1310nm laser and APD receiver;
- 4x25.78Gb/s with NRZ electrical interface (CAUI-4);
- I2C interface with integrated Digital Diagnostic monitoring;
- QSFP28 MSA package with LC duplex connector;
- Single +3.3V power supply;
- Maximum power consumption 4.5 W;
- Operating case temperature: 0 to +70 °C;
- Compliant to 802.3cd, SFF-8636&amp;SFF-8679 standard;
- Complies with EU Directive 2015/863/EU;

## Application

- 100GBASE-ER4;
- Data center / Cloud application;
- Other application.
